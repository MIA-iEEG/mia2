These are the hands-on sessions given around MIA and Brainstorm. The lectures
introduce the method, the walkthroughs are the step-by-step documents followed
during the session.

The material reflects the version of MIA taught at the time, so it may differ
from the current interface. For the current workflow, follow the
[tutorial]({{ '/tutorial/' | relative_url }}).
